Where it started

Most IT staffing firms rely on speed and volume—flooding clients with resumes and hoping something sticks. It’s a race to the bottom that prioritizes quantity over fit.

At The Hatch Group, we saw an opportunity to raise the bar.

 

Solving for better staffing

We set out to transform the IT staffing experience by listening to our clients, learning from real data, and creating a process that delivers true value—not just warm bodies in seats.

Introducing IVP

That process is called IVP: Identify, Validate, Prescribe. Built around independent subject matter experts (not internal staff), IVP ensures every consultant is evaluated by someone who is objective, has no ulterior motive and has "been there, done that."

A QUICK BACKSTORY

(Because you deserve to know who you’re working with)

We started The Hatch Group in 2017, not with a grand plan, but with a simple one: build a company that reflects how we actually like to work—with integrity, clarity, and curiosity.

The name comes from our love of fly fishing (yep, really). A “hatch” is that moment on the water when things align, and everything clicks. We like to think that’s what it feels like when the right candidate meets the right company, too.

That’s it. No big speeches. Just a little context for who’s behind the work.
